# Tableau--Retail-Sales-and-Demand-Forecasting-Dashboard
Interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing retail sales performance ,inventory trends, and demand forecasting across product categories and regions.
This tableau dashboard provides comprehensive insights into retail sales performance ,inventory management ,and demand forcasting. it enables businesses to monitor sales ,and optimize inventory level across product categories and regions.
## Key features
- Total Revenue and Total Sales Analysis.
- Inventory Turnover Ratio Monitoring.
- Order Quantity Trends Analysis.
- Category-wise Inventory Forcasting Comprisons,Impact of Seasonality and Weather Conditions on Sales.
- Impact of Seasonality and Weather Conditions on Sales.
- Sales performance Trend over time.
- Demand Forcast vs Unit Sold Analysis.
- Revenue by product Category
- Interactive Filters For Year ,Category,Region,and Seasonality
## Tools &Technologies Used:
 * Tableau
 * Data Visualization
 * Calculated Fields
 * Filters and Parameters
## Business Objective:
This Dashboard helps organization understand sales pattern,forecast demand, optimize inventory and support strategic decision making through data-driven insights
